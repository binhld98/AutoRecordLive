# LiveCatch

LiveCatch records live broadcasts from channels a user has added, so the user can export footage from them later. This glossary fixes the words used in docs, UI text, routes, and code.

## Language

### Channels

**Platform**:
A streaming service LiveCatch records from: TikTok or Douyin.
_Avoid_: Site, source

**Channel**:
An account on a platform that broadcasts live. It is the thing a user adds, and LiveCatch checks and records.
_Avoid_: Host, streamer, creator, account

**Host**:
A person presenting on a channel. One channel can have several hosts, and LiveCatch does not track them.
_Avoid_: Streamer

**Add**:
To save a channel to the user's own list. Adding alone records nothing.
_Avoid_: Follow, subscribe, save

**Remove**:
To delete a channel from the user's list. Its recordings stay until they expire.
_Avoid_: Unfollow, delete

**Listening**:
The per-channel setting that makes LiveCatch record every broadcast of that channel automatically. A channel in the user's list is either listening or not listening.
_Avoid_: Monitoring, watching, auto-record, tracking

**Listener**:
One listening slot of the user's plan. Each listening channel holds one, and the plan's listener limit caps how many channels listen at once.
_Avoid_: Slot, seat, monitor

**Broadcast**:
One live session of a channel, from when it goes live until it ends.
_Avoid_: Live stream, stream, live

**Live**:
The state of a channel while a broadcast is running.
_Avoid_: Online, streaming

**Check**:
One look at a channel on its platform to learn whether it is live.
_Avoid_: Poll, ping, scan

### Recordings and plans

**Recording**:
The footage LiveCatch captured from one broadcast of a channel for one user.
_Avoid_: Video, capture, stream

**Recording hours**:
The plan's monthly allowance of captured footage, summed across channels.
_Avoid_: Minutes, quota, credits

**Plan**:
A paid subscription tier that limits recording hours, how many channels listen at once, and how long recordings are kept.
_Avoid_: Package, tier, subscription

**Trial**:
The time-limited free period every new user starts on. It has its own limits and is not a plan.
_Avoid_: Free plan, free tier
